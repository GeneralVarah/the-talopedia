**Arverne**, officially the United Regions of Arverne, is a country in Southern Evria, bordering Ymiria to the north, Saint Testudera to the southwest, and Vicily to the southeast. It is the 5th most populous nation on the continent and possesses the world's 5th largest GDP (PPP). The nation boasts a diverse climate and geography, including temperate Cantabrian coasts, central plains, and even high mountain regions. This, historically, has allowed Arverne to be a significant economic and political power on the continent. During the Napolyonic Wars, Arverne served as a continental hegemony for almost half a century. Arverne is a presidential republic currently led by President Henri Charbonneau, leading the centre-left Montagnard Party in the National Assembly. Arverne is primarily made up of ethnic Arvernois people; however, the nation is host to multiple other groups, the largest of which is the Cachelan peoples in Callete.
