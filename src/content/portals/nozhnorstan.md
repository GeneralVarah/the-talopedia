**Nozhnorstan** (Ножнорстан, Noʐnərstən), officially the Republic of Nozhnorstan (Ножнория Режапубликая, Aldrige: Nozhnoriya Rezhapublikaya, Nɐʐnɐˈrʲijə Rʲɪʐəpʊˈblʲikəjə), is a country in Northeastern Valtheria. It overlooks for a small southeastern coastline on the Himalayan Sea, while bordering with Perovska and Rudania to the Southwest, Axerfreditenshin to the West and Furan to the North. Notwithstanding the isolated position of the country given by the northern and western mountain ranges, it has a considerable population of 17,490,000 circa. The capital of Nozhnorstan is Petrilovzhka, with 3,416,000 inhabitants.
