**Soldiers Without Borders**, often shortened to **SWB**, is a private military company founded and led by former Elysian intelligence operative [[goro-kurotowa]] starting in the early 1930’s. Untied to any nation or government, the volunteer fighters of the organization, the so-called “soldiers without borders”, provide their services to all clients, regardless of nation or ideology, operating out of their island base in the Southern Hyperborean Ocean. The group was formed as a result of the dismantling of MAJESTY by the Allied Powers, essentially acting as a continuation of the organization under Kurotowa’s leadership.
