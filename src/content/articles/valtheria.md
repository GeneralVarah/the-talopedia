**Valtheria** (/vælˈθɪəriə/, *VAL-theer-ee-uh*) is [[avium]]’s most populated and second-largest continent. In general terms, it is bounded on the west by the Hyperborean Ocean, on the north and east by the Eastern Panthalassan Ocean, and on the south by the watershed of the Ural Mountains. Valtheria exhibits immense diversity both between and within its various regions, encompassing a wide array of ethnicities, cultures, and ecosystems. Climatically, the continent spans several extremes, ranging from subarctic in Spatrya and Western [[nichirin]], tropical monsoon in the Ma Suli Peninsula, to semi-arid in its interior.
