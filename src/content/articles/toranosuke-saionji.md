**Toranosuke Saionji** (西園寺 寅之助; born January 21, 1859) is a [[nichirin|Nichirian]] military officer serving as Minister of Defense under Shogun [[masashi-miyamoto]], a position he has held since December 1921. Saionji trained at the [[shinkeisei]] Military Academy and the National War College before receiving his commission in the late 1870s, during the period of centralized reform that followed the Bakuraku War. He served in the [[shogunate-ground-corps]] through the dismantlement of the han system and the subsequent modernization of Nichirin's military, rising over three decades from a junior posting to the senior ranks of the officer corps. He was appointed Marshal-General of the Bulwark, the highest field command in the Nichirian army, on July 15, 1910.

During [[great-war|the Great War]], Saionji commanded the ground campaign against the Xing Empire in the Valtherian theater. His approach favored positional defense in depth, ceding territory to allow Xing forces to overextend their supply lines before committing to a counteroffensive once naval operations under Marshal-Admiral [[suiren-makinami]] had severed the enemy's maritime supply capacity. The Defense of [[mizuhara]], in which General Mayu Kitta held the city against a sustained Xing offensive, was conducted under Saionji's overall strategic command. Three of his five children were killed in military service during the conflict.

He relinquished command to his successor, Marshal-General Kiyoshi Kiryū, in August 1918 and entered semi-retirement before his appointment as Minister of Defense in December 1921. At seventy-two, he is the longest-serving and oldest member of the current cabinet, with more than six decades of military service. He is married to Makiko Kamiyoshi and has two surviving children, Kunihiko and Shōko.
