**Noboru Wakabayashi** (若林 昇; born September 1, 1886) is a [[nichirin|Nichirian]] politician serving as Minister of Agriculture under Shogun [[masashi-miyamoto]]. Wakabayashi studied political science at [[shinkeisei]] Polytechnical University before entering regional administration, serving as Governor of Aomizu Prefecture in Manshu from June 1924 to February 1927. Aomizu lies within the Great Manshuan Plain, Nichirin's principal agricultural territory, and Wakabayashi's governorship provided direct administrative experience in the region whose output his ministry now oversees. He was appointed Minister of Agriculture on March 1, 1927, succeeding Sukenobu Sera, and is the only member of the current cabinet to have held a prefectural governorship.
