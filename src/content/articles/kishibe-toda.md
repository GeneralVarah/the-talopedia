**Kishibe Toda** (戸田 岸辺; June 11, 1885 – February 6, 1930) was a [[nichirin|Nichirian]] economist who served as Minister of Economy, Trade and Industry under Shoguns Masanori and [[masashi-miyamoto]] from May 1922 until his death. Toda studied economics at the University of [[shinkeisei]], completing a Bachelor of Science and a Master of Business Administration, before beginning his career at the Izumiya zaibatsu, where he transitioned from a commercial role into economic policy work before his appointment to the ministry, succeeding Takayuki Furuta. In January 1930, Toda identified irregularities in stevedore overtime records at Shinkeisei's port facilities, an investigation that exposed a shipping fraud operation connected to Kiyonari Tsujimura's organization on the Shizuku Riverfront. He was killed on February 6, 1930, during an armed siege of a restricted government facility in Shiratsuki, Shinkeisei.
