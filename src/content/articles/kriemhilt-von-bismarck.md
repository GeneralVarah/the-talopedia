**Hildegard Kriemhilt Adelheid von Bismarck** (クリームヒルト・フォン・ビスマルク; born February 14, 1898) is an [[alemannia|Alemannian]] politician serving as the High Commissioner of the Aureate Co-Prosperity Sphere (光輪共栄圏, *Kōrin Kyōeiken*) under Shogun [[masashi-miyamoto]]. The daughter of Alexander von Bismarck, a Ymirian noble with longstanding ties to the Nichirian government, she completed a Bachelor of Science in colonial administration at KYPS Akenau before the outbreak of the [[holy-evrian-civil-war]] in May 1918. Her father arranged her dispatch to Nichirin under an agreement with Shogun Masanori Miyamoto during the conflict, which dissolved the [[holy-evrian-empire]] into its successor states over the following six months.

In Nichirin, she served as an attaché to the Ymirian Advisory Mission while completing a Master of Science in colonial policy at Takarazuka Imperial University. During her attachment to the Ministry of Colonial Affairs, she produced analytical work on colonial governance that the ministry had not requested; the work drew sufficient attention that Shogun Masashi Miyamoto appointed her Minister of Colonial Affairs directly on October 13, 1924, following the death of her predecessor Shosuke Kaga. At twenty-six, she was the youngest ministerial appointment of Masashi's tenure and remains the only foreign national in the cabinet.

Von Bismarck's ministry administers Nichirin's colonial territories, including Hōrai and Kinshū, with a combined population exceeding 150 million. In April 1930, she attended the Akenau Conference in Arlbern alongside Chief Liaison Officer Akane Shikinami-Hoffmann. Her expertise in Alemannian political history has informed the cabinet's assessment of the Akenauer Bund, the coordination framework between Ymiria, Arlbern, and Nordmark, which she has described as a reunification vehicle for the former Holy Evrian successor states.
