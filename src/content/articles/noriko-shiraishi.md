**Noriko Shiraishi** (白石 典子; born November 21, 1885) is a [[nichirin|Nichirian]] physician and epidemiologist serving as Minister of Health under Shogun [[masashi-miyamoto]], a position she has held since October 1917. Shiraishi studied biology at the University of [[naginomiya]] before completing her medical degree and doctorate in epidemiology at Kyowa University. Prior to her ministerial appointment, she served as a senior research fellow at the [[seele]] Scientific Division, where she worked alongside Dr. Yui Shinazugawa, before departing under circumstances that have not been publicly disclosed. She was appointed Minister of Health during [[great-war|the Great War]], and her thirteen-year tenure spans the administrations of both Masanori and Masashi Miyamoto, making her one of the longest-serving members of the current cabinet alongside Minister of Defense [[toranosuke-saionji]]. Shiraishi has used a wheelchair since childhood due to transverse myelitis.
