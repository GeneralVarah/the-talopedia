**Gestaltism** (from Alemannian *Gestalt* ‘shape, figure, form’) is a political and economic ideology whose goal is the creation of a unified human society, a singular human political entity with a near-omnipotent central authority figure to govern over mankind. A Gestaltist society entails the eventual absence of private property, social classes, money and ultimately, the state itself. Gestaltism is part of the broader socialist movement.

Unlike most other socialist movements across the world, Gestaltism considers economic policies secondary, with Gestaltist writer [[archer-maine]] seeing them as tools, where “private enterprises can be controlled, public enterprises can be controlled better”. Gestaltists derive their belief for a strong central authority figure with near omnipresent surveillance of mankind from a religious basis.
