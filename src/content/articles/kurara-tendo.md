**Kurara Tendo** (天童 空良; born December 31, 1893) is a [[nichirin|Nichirian]] academic serving as Minister of Education under Shogun [[masashi-miyamoto]]. Born in [[mizuhara]], Tendo studied literature at [[takarazuka]] Imperial University, where she completed a Bachelor of Arts, a Master of Arts in classical Nichirian literature, and a doctorate in educational philosophy. She joined the Takarazuka faculty as a lecturer and later professor before her appointment as Minister of Education on July 4, 1928, succeeding Tokimasa Ishida. Her approach to curriculum policy, rooted in classical Nichirian literary tradition, has produced outcomes her colleagues have found difficult to anticipate and harder to dispute.
